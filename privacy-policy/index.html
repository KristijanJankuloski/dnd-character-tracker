Privacy Policy

JJs Character Tracker (dev.kristijanj.charactertracker)
Last updated: 2026-11-11

Summary

The app does not collect, upload or sell your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers operated by the developer.

Data stored on your device

Characters and related notes you create are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Camera

The camera permission is used only to scan QR codes when you pair devices for sync. Camera images are processed on the device, and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Device-to-device sync

If you choose to sync, character data is exchanged directly between your own devices over your local network (Wi-Fi). It does not pass through any server owned by the developer, and the developer cannot see it.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services and does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
